About the event

One Uganda. One stage.

WordCamp Uganda 2027 brings developers, designers, content creators and businesses from Uganda and around the world together in Kampala, the capital of Uganda in East Africa.

WordCamps are informal, volunteer-organised conferences held around the world for anyone who uses, builds or makes a living with WordPress, the open-source software behind millions of websites. Expect practical talks, open conversations and new connections.
